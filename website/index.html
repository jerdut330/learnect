Questions

Prices, refunds and what works today.

Full refundUntil 24 hours before No refundLast 24 hours

Can I book a real session today?

Not yet. LearNect is a student venture. The demo for the 25 November 2026 showcase uses made-up people and takes no real payment.

How much does a session cost?

Rp20.000 to Rp30.000 per person, shown on every session.

What if a session is cancelled?

Cancel at least 24 hours before for a full refund. Later cancellations are non-refundable. If your mentor cancels, you get a full refund.

Who can teach on LearNect?

BINUS students who apply and are approved. Mentors keep 80% of each completed booking.

Does LearNect read my KRS?

Not yet. The demo uses a filled-in class schedule for one student, Alex.
